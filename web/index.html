Why Nexus

Built for the complexity
of hyperscale EPC.

📄

Intelligent Document Ingestion

Thousands of tender pages, bid responses, and policy documents are scanned, classified, and indexed into a searchable knowledge base with tamper-evident audit trails.

🔍

Zero-Miss Bid Verification

Every numeric requirement, tolerance, unit, and standard reference is checked algorithmically. Complex clauses get LLM reasoning — but only a rule-based pass can auto-clear.

⚠️

Predictive Risk Forecasting

Five specialised sub-agents monitor procurement, logistics, weather, workforce, and vendor history. Slip propagates along the critical path with ranked mitigations.

✅

Automated Commissioning QA

Test cases are generated from IST procedures and split into machine-verifiable and engineer-only checks. Failures auto-open NCRs with cited precedents from past projects.

Architecture

From raw documents
to audit-ready commissioning.

01

Foundation Layer

Documents are ingested via AWS Textract, classified by taxonomy, and stored in a hash-chained audit log. A TF-IDF vector index powers clause-level RAG retrieval across projects.

S3TextractSQLite → AuroraHash Chain
02

Engine 1 — Verification

Vendor bids are compared against the tender clause by clause. Numeric checks (voltage, efficiency, transfer time) are deterministic. LLM reasoning is recorded but never auto-clears — human review is always required for ambiguous findings.

Rule EngineClaude Opus 5Fulfilment Score
03

Engine 2 — Risk Assessment

Sub-agents analyse procurement lead times, port congestion, monsoon forecasts, workforce availability, and vendor track records. Signals fuse into a risk score per activity, and slip propagates along the critical path with actionable mitigations.

5 Sub-AgentsCritical PathMonte Carlo
04

Engine 3 — Commissioning QA

IST procedures (TIA-942, BICSI, Uptime Tier) generate test cases, auto-split into machine-verifiable and engineer-only. Historian telemetry executes automated checks; failures open NCRs with RAG-sourced precedent resolutions.

Auto/Manual SplitNCR + PrecedentEventBridge
05

Integration & Event Bus

All engines communicate via an event bus. An NCR in commissioning instantly lowers the vendor's score in verification. A late shipment holds commissioning cases. A resolved NCR becomes a precedent for the next project.

EventBridgeDynamoDB AuditCross-Engine

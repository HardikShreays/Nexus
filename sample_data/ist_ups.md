# IST Procedure — Electrical / UPS, Data Hall 1 (rev C)
## UPS
[IST-UPS-01] UPS transfer time on mains failure <= 4 ms
[IST-UPS-02] UPS output voltage 415 V ±1% under 100% load step
[IST-UPS-03] UPS battery autonomy >= 10 min at full load
[IST-UPS-04] UPS output voltage THD <= 3 %
[IST-UPS-05] Visual inspection of UPS cabinet terminations and torque marking
[IST-UPS-06] Thermographic scan of UPS input breaker connections at full load, hotspot < 70 °C
[IST-UPS-07] Verify UPS redundancy N+1 by isolating one module under load
[IST-UPS-08] Emergency power off (EPO) test witnessed by client representative
## Generator
[IST-DG-01] Generator start and accept load time <= 10 s
